PROPOSED SOLUTION
HOW IT ADDRESSESS PS 33

INNOVATION & UNIQUENESS
Kisan Direct is an AI-assisted digital agricultural marketplace connecting farmers and FPOs directly with verified retailers, restaurants, processors and institutional buyers

Kisan Direct  improves price discovery, intelligently matches farmers with suitable buyers, forecasts demand, and optimizes logistics to reduce unnecessary supply-chain losses and improve value realization for farmers and buyers.

FARMER → LIST → AI MATCH → PRICE → BUYER → LOGISTICS → DELIVERY → PAYMENT


Direct crop listings and verified buyer offers reduce opaque, unnecessary layers.

Price Intelligence provides market context, trends and clearly labelled estimates before negotiation.

Explainable Al competes crop, quantity, quality, price, distance. delivery and reliability.

Logistics estimates Show vehicle, cost and ETA before the farmer accepts.

Farmer remains in control: Al recommends and explains. but never auto-accepts.


Explainable match score: Instead of a black-box recommendation, every buyer-match result includes sub-scores, reasons and warnings. 

 Beyond highest price: A nearer buyer with a fair offer can rank higher than a distant buyer whose logistics cost erodes the apparent gain.

Modular AI: Matching, price prediction, demand forecasting and logistics are independently runnable Python modules, making them replaceable without changing the API contract.

Transparent service layer: The design retains logistics and aggregation as visible services rather than treating them as a problem to eliminate.

TECHNOLOGIES TO BE USED

METHODOLOGY & WORKFLOW
        Complete transaction flow
 Register and authenticate: Farmer, buyer or administrator signs in through JWT-backed role access. 

Create a listing: Farmer/FPO enters crop, quantity, quality, expected price, harvest timing and location. 

Discover and offer: Buyer searches marketplace listings and submits an offer for a suitable batch. 

Compute match: System scores crop fit, quantity, quality, price, distance, delivery compatibility and reliability.

Review explanation: Farmer sees overall score, factor-level reasons, warnings and the logistics implications.

Plan delivery: Logistics service estimates distance, vehicle type, cost and ETA for the route.

TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY

Core MVP already works on mature React, PostgreSQL, Docker and JWT technologies.
Modular ML services can improve without enhancing the main API contract.

MARKET FEASIBILITY

Start with one crop cluster , FPO networks and anchor buyers rather than nationwide liquidity
Farmers need price context and buyer access ; buyers need verified batches and fulfilment visibility.
OPERATIONAL FEASIBILITY

FPOs Support onboarding, aegregation and quality checks.
Logistics providers remain visible , priced services partners.
COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES

KisanDirect differentiates through one integrated, farmer-controlled workflow
Combines prices intelligence , demand forecast , explainable offer matching and logistics in one flow.
VALIDATION

Run 1-3 FPO clusters with selected crops and anchor buyers. Verify onboarding completion, active listings, offer response time and order completion.
AI validation Compare match recommendations with actual accepted offers and fulfilled orders. 
Economic validation Compare farmer net realization, buyer procurement cost, logistics cost and wastage proxies against the baseline; control for crop, grade, season and distance.

IMPACT AND BENEFITS
5
@SIH Idea submission- Template
TEAM ALLIED
TARGET AUDIENCE & REACH

Small and marginal farmers, FPOs, SHGs, retailers, restaurants, processors, institutions and logistics providers.
Begin with district-level crop clusters and expand through repeat buyers and FPO Partnership
SOCIAL IMPACT

Strengthens farmer agency through clear offer, price, quality and delivery information.
Supports structured buyer discovery for FPOs and women-led SHGs.
Builds a traceable transaction record for clearer dispute handling
ECONOMIC IMPACT

Farmers can compare feasible offers using transparent price and logistics information rather than a single local option.
Buyers gain a more structured way to source batches that fit quality, quantity and timing requirements.
ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

Demand-aware matching and route estimates can reduce failed deliveries and spoilage risk.
Future multi-stop routing can reduce avoidable travel.

SCALABILITY & SUSTAINABILITY

Phase 1: 1-3 FPO clusters, selected perishables, assisted onboarding, few anchor buyers, manual operations support.

Phase 2: Standardized verification, trained FPO champions, buyer SLA, route-partner network and formal performance dashboard.

Phase 3: Multiple crop clusters, localized demand models, state-specific compliance and integrations with verified market-data sources.

Phase 4: Road-distance routing, multi-stop optimization.
